# From competitors to marketing decisions

## Start with what the user wants to produce

Specify audience, market, language, input, desired output and the client's verified capabilities. For a marketing baseline, sample a direct product, a value-chain neighbor and a publisher or creator that reaches the user. Verify an actual page, video, listing or discussion for each material content competitor. Keep one evidence register and derive channel and product views from it.

For long-form video generation, start at the destination: inspect YouTube categories, visible tags, titles, playlists, descriptions and credits. Separate platform labels from analyst groupings. Sample news and explainers, sports recaps or analysis, films or film commentary, music videos, lyric videos and visualizers as relevant. Follow a real example back through its tutorial, template, assets and tools. Look upstream at model interfaces and effect galleries such as Vidu or PixVerse; inspect named effects, examples, prompts and available public landing pages. A usable effect inside a product suggests a query to validate even when no dedicated page is found in the inspected sample. Record URL inventory checked, competing publishers and whether the client can deliver the result.

## Extract and follow discoveries

Inspect navigation, URL families, top pages when data exists, template fields, job-specific pages, Why Choose claims, help topics and conversion links. For an unexpected page, record the original expectation, observed user action, artifact and next step. Check related pages or product behavior before inferring a recurring use case. For a proposed series, compare representative siblings: identify the fixed task, variable entity and unique content or action each instance offers. Capture a feature or creator phrase, search it independently and inspect the new domain or native listing. Return newly discovered entities to the shared register. Stop when additional traces repeat known tasks or the agreed boundary is reached.

## Product choice and competitive events

Test substitution: would choosing the candidate reduce, delay or replace the client product for the specified job? Classify relationship separately from whole versus partial scope. Whole-product competition requires matching core positioning and coverage of all major tasks; a broad platform can overlap a specialist only on one use case. Compare the same input and desired result across alternatives, including manual services, direct generation, node workflows and agentic execution where relevant. Examine actual controls, quality, price unit, export, portability and switching costs. Mark what was tested, vendor-stated or unknown. Separate user reviews and vendor positioning from independently observed outcomes.

For closure, acquisition, plan, feature, license or API changes, verify the announcement, effective date and affected users. Distinguish a timely response, a decision aid, migration instructions and actual import/compatibility support. Check export format, field mapping, limitations and recovery before proposing migration content.

## Deliverables and handoff

Lead with findings that change a marketing decision. For each material opportunity give the audience and task, representative competitor evidence with date and locale, proposed asset or page family, client capability, relation to existing pages and next validation. Consider feature pages, model or effect pages, templates, galleries, workflow guides, comparisons and creator distribution. A visible feature, a ranking, a backlink and an AI citation support different claims. Use metrics only with a source and measurement window. Treat an unsupported product promise as a market observation. Keep the discovery path visible and state unchecked surfaces.

Pass keyword-research the observed URL or native asset, user action, inferred need and candidate query. It validates language, same-page clusters and destinations. Feed newly discovered domains back into competitor research.

When an existing site's inventory is missing or uncertain, use [site-structure-inventory](https://github.com/kostja94/vibe-coding/tree/main/skills/site-structure-inventory) to verify live pages, URL families and discovery gaps before making recommendations.
