# Category research

Deliver a demand map and a seed portfolio grouped by task. Begin with the category and target market. Treat known vendors, products, roles, jobs, technologies, quality constraints and adjacent workflows as leads. Verify brand identity. Distinguish direct competitors, substitutes, upstream suppliers and complementary tools.

Select contrasting products. Inspect public navigation and internal links, templates, use cases, feature promises, comparisons, alternatives, migration and help pages. Use top-page performance data when available; a navigation link alone indicates coverage. Look for surprising pages outside obvious category terms. Follow at least one material discovery into a second search using a specific job or feature, then inspect a newly found provider. Retain the trail: source URL, observed fact, inferred user task, next query, new evidence. Report inaccessible sources.

For product surfaces, inspect what users supply, receive and do next. For each promising repeatable series identify its fixed task, varying entity, genuinely unique data or workflow, and a representative URL. Compare examples before recommending scale. Consider a filterable directory, leaderboard, comparison, template or interactive action when that matches the task. Vendor differentiators suggest hypotheses to validate against user language. Distinguish market terminology from emerging category-name proposals.

Group findings by user task and deliverable. For each material branch give audience, evidence, representative seed queries, competitor, potential asset and confidence. Select representative contrasting branches for deeper keyword-discovery and clustering; label remaining branches as leads. Default to an unranked map for US research; apply the entrypoint rules for any requested ranking. Separate evidence of demand from product fit, repeat use and willingness to pay. Stop when additional exploration repeats existing tasks or reaches the agreed boundary; state the largest gap. Keep the report proportional to evidence.
