# Landing-page product paths

Classify pages by their actual job: homepage, capability, feature, use case, persona, team size, industry, enterprise, pricing, comparison, alternative, customer story, API, program, registration, login or app entry. Resolve overlapping intents as an architecture question before prescribing links.

Build candidates from supported product relationships. Pair a feature with the use cases it serves; connect personas to workflows and proof, industries to requirements and evidence, cases to capabilities actually used, pricing tiers to included features, comparisons to differentiators or migration paths, and program pages to participation. Evaluate each direction independently. An empty matrix cell or a shared label creates no requirement.

Inspect navigation, homepage sections, feature and scenario cards, persona entrances, case summaries, plan items and integrations. If a reader needs details that lack a destination, record a missing-node proposal and check there is enough distinct content to justify a page. Use a crawlable semantic link even when the entire card is clickable.

Preserve one primary next action and a small set of supporting paths. Use a Blog link when a tutorial, explanation, study or case helps the decision, with lower visual priority than the main CTA. Confirm commercial pages have appropriate product or hub entrances beyond Blog or sitemap, and check each destination's locale, current capabilities and intended action.
